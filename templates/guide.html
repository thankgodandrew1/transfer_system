User walkthrough

From iMOS export to Transfer News and movement plan.

Generate the verified news first, then compare it with the previous cycle to prepare driver movements. Use sanitized sample files when demonstrating the system publicly.

Workflow one

Generate Transfer News

  1. 01

    Export the four records

    Prepare the current Transfer Management PDF, previous Transfer News PDF, current roster report, and previous roster report.

  2. 02

    Upload and confirm details

    Select each file, verify the mission and leadership names, and override the title only when automatic detection is unsuitable.

  3. 03

    Generate securely

    Keep the browser tab open while the system parses, matches, verifies, and renders the package.

  4. 04

    Review corrections and warnings

    Open the workbook, inspect the Verification and Changes sheets, and read any warnings on the results page.

  5. 05

    Download and continue the cycle

    Download the ZIP. Keep the updated-roster PDF because it becomes the previous Transfer News input next cycle.

Workflow two

Generate the Transfer Movement plan

Open Movement plan after the current Transfer News has been approved.

  1. 01

    Upload the two Transfer News files

    Use the previous cycle as the FROM record and the newly approved Transfer News as the current record. Paste a Missionary, Zone, Area table in the fallback panel only if a PDF cannot be parsed.

  2. 02

    Upload the apartment directory

    CSV or XLSX is recommended for office staff. JSON and the legacy Word list also work. Every row needs a Zone, Apartment, and Area.

  3. 03

    Add genuine historical exceptions

    Enter only confirmed exceptions in the form Elder Twum: Anua Obio -> Mbierebe. The normal mapping handles every other change.

  4. 04

    Generate and review

    The system skips new arrivals and released missionaries, groups movements by the previous zone, and stops with an exact list if any transferred area is missing from the directory.

  5. 05

    Give the Word plan to the drivers

    The STATUS cells are intentionally blank. Drivers tick or mark each row only after that missionary has moved successfully. Keep the returned editable directory CSV for the next cycle.
