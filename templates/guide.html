User walkthrough

From iMOS export to finished Transfer News.

Follow the five steps below. Use sanitized sample files when demonstrating the system publicly.

  1. 01

    Export the four records

    Prepare the current Transfer Management PDF, previous Transfer News PDF, current roster report, and previous roster report.

  2. 02

    Upload and confirm details

    Select each file, verify the mission and leadership names, and override the title only when automatic detection is unsuitable.

  3. 03

    Generate securely

    Keep the browser tab open while the system parses, matches, verifies, and renders the package.

  4. 04

    Review corrections and warnings

    Open the workbook, inspect the Verification and Changes sheets, and read any warnings on the results page.

  5. 05

    Download and continue the cycle

    Download the ZIP. Keep the updated-roster PDF because it becomes the previous Transfer News input next cycle.

Privacy reminder

Never place real transfer files in GitHub, email attachments, tutorial recordings, or a public support request.
